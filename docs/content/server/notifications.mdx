---
title: Notifications
description: Get alerted by webhook, push notification or email.
---

Set up notifications in **Settings → Integrations**. Every configured channel receives every message, and every message is also kept in the dashboard, behind the [bell](#the-bell), even when no channel is set up.

## Channels

| Channel | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| **Webhook** | A Discord or Slack-compatible webhook URL. |
| **ntfy** | An [ntfy](https://ntfy.sh) topic URL, such as `https://ntfy.sh/my-quasar-topic`. Push notifications on a phone, without a chat service. |
| **Email** | An SMTP server: host, port, sender, recipients (comma-separated), and optionally a username and password. |

Leave a channel empty to turn it off.

After any change, click **Send test**. A broken webhook looks exactly like a quiet server.

## What triggers a notification

- A deployment fails.
- An application goes into an error state, and when it recovers.
- A [health check](/applications/resources-health/#health-checks) fails 3 times and the app is restarted.
- A scheduled [task](/applications/automation/#tasks) fails.
- A backup fails, or its offsite upload fails.
- The server crosses a usage threshold (see below), and when it goes back under.
- A new Quasar version is available.
- [Updates](/server/updates/) are waiting on the server.
- A [station](/stations/permissions/#notify) sends a message.

## Usage alerts

Under **Server usage alerts**, set thresholds for **disk**, **memory** and **CPU**, in percent.

You are notified once when a threshold is crossed, and once when usage goes back under it. Set a threshold to `0` to turn it off.

## In the dashboard

The outcome of what you do in the dashboard shows as a notification in the top-right corner of the page: green when it worked, in the accent colour when something has started, orange for a warning, red when it failed. Successes close after a few seconds, warnings a little later, and errors stay until you close them. A notification stays on screen when you move to another page, and its timer pauses while the pointer is on it.

Long actions run in the background: a [cleanup](/server/disk/#cleaning-up), a [backup or a restore](/server/backups/), a deployment, a [server update](/server/updates/). You can leave the page; when it ends, a notification says how it went on whichever page you are on. Only the person who started it sees it.

## The bell

The bell in the header keeps what notifications said once they are gone:

- every alert listed [above](#what-triggers-a-notification), for everybody who signs in;
- how the long actions you started ended, for you only.

Its number counts what arrived since you last opened it. Opening it marks everything as read; what was new stays marked with a dot until you open it again. Click a notification to go to the page it is about.

The tabs show **All**, only the **Alerts**, or only yours (**Mine**). **See all** opens the **Notifications** page, which lists the whole history and filters it by kind: errors, warnings, info, successes.

Notifications are kept 30 days.
