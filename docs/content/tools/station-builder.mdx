Seven steps, one at a time. On the right, the page your station will produce, or the document it writes — the two update as you type, and the checks flag what Quasar would refuse at install.

At the end, **download** or **copy** the document, then add it in **Settings → Stations** in your dashboard. See [installing a station](/stations/installing/).
