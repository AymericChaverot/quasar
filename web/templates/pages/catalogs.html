What a catalogue looks like

Two levels of substitution meet in an entry, and the difference is the thing to hold on to: {{"{{NAME}}"}} is answered when the entry is picked and substituted by Quasar, while ${NAME} is left alone for docker compose to read from the .env at run time. Quasar also answers {{"{{RANDOM}}"}}, {{"{{HOST}}"}} and {{"{{URL}}"}} by itself.
