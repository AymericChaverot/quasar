Stations

A station is an application that arrives with a control surface of its own — tabs, actions and views somebody wrote for one service in particular. It deploys like a catalogue entry and produces an ordinary application; what it adds is the page you get afterwards.

It is also a program somebody else wrote, so what it may reach on this server is declared in its own document — a list of services, paths, environment keys and addresses, each one named. You are shown that list in plain words here and accept it, or you do not install the station; there is nothing to configure per application afterwards. A revision that asks for anything more is held, and keeps running the one you accepted until you accept the new list.
